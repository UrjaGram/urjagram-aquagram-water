Results

Purpose

  • Capture the current dairy process of the village's cow-owning families and its economics.
  • Offer three cumulative investment stages and assess the increase in household income and the reduction in greenhouse gases.
  • Serve as a common basis for discussion with households, government departments, CSR agencies, philanthropies, banks and carbon buyers.

The three stages

  • Stage 1 — Herd productivity. Breeding and AI, health and para-vet cadre, balanced feed with minerals, year-round fodder through silage and fodder trees, water at the shed.
  • Stage 2 — Biogas. Family plants for households with four or more animals, a community plant for the rest of the dung; LPG and firewood displaced, biofertiliser used and sold.
  • Stage 3 — Milk processing. Collection centre, bulk cooler and a products plant; price realisation, lower spoilage, value added.

Stages are cumulative: 1 → 1+2 → 1+2+3. Tree planting to offset the residual sits outside the stages and is assumed grant- or scheme-funded.

Data status. Herd count and household segments come from the panchayat livestock register (906 animals, 82 owners; 94% of records pre-2023). All other parameters are assumptions pending the field survey. The two milk prices (₹40 local, ₹60 aggregated) and the 4 L/day yield move the answer most — verify these first.
